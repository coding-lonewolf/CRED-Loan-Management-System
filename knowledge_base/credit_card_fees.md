# Credit-Card Fee Structure

This fictional knowledge-base entry describes common fee categories rather than a specific issuer's tariff. Credit-card charges may include annual or joining fees, cash-advance fees, late-payment charges, over-limit fees where permitted, foreign-currency markup, and interest on unpaid balances. The actual amount, tax treatment, waiver conditions, and effective date must be checked against the issuer's current schedule of charges and cardholder agreement. Never invent a fee amount when the approved schedule is unavailable.


Issuing Institution: Apex National Bank (Fictional)
Product Name: Apex Standard Rewards Credit Card
Card Type: Standard / Entry-Level Credit Card
Currency: Indian Rupee (INR)
Effective Date: 01 April 2026
Document Type: Product Fee Schedule
Version: 1.0
Important: This is a fictional product fee schedule created for a banking knowledge-base and capstone project. The values are illustrative and are not an actual tariff published by any financial institution.

1. Joining and Annual Membership Fees
- Joining Fee: ₹999 (one-time, charged upon card issuance).
- Annual Membership Fee: ₹999 per year, beginning from the second year.
- Annual Fee Waiver: The annual fee will be waived if the cardholder completes eligible retail transactions worth ₹1,50,000 or more during the preceding membership year.
- Supplementary Card Fee: ₹299 per additional card annually.
2. Cash Advance Charges
- Cash Withdrawal Fee: 2.5% of the withdrawn amount, subject to a minimum charge of ₹500 per transaction.
- Cash Advance Interest Rate: 3.50% per month (42% annually).
- Interest Calculation: Interest will accrue from the cash withdrawal transaction date until the outstanding cash advance is fully repaid.
- Interest-Free Period: Cash withdrawals are not eligible for an interest-free period.
3. Late Payment Charges
Late payment fees are calculated based on the total outstanding statement balance remaining unpaid after the payment due date.
Outstanding Amount	Late Payment Fee
₹0–₹500	Nil
₹501–₹5,000	₹400
₹5,001–₹10,000	₹500
₹10,001–₹20,000	₹750
Above ₹20,000	₹1,000


Late payment charges are applied once per billing cycle, subject to applicable regulatory requirements.
4. Over-Limit Charges
- Over-Limit Fee: 2.5% of the excess amount, subject to a minimum charge of ₹500.
- Applicability: Charged only where an over-limit facility has been permitted and the cardholder has provided the required consent.
- The bank may decline transactions exceeding the approved credit limit.
5. Foreign Currency Transaction Charges
- Foreign Currency Markup: 3.50% of the converted INR transaction amount.
- Applicable Transactions: International purchases, overseas ATM withdrawals, and transactions processed in foreign currencies.
- Currency Conversion: The applicable card network exchange rate will be used for currency conversion.
- Additional taxes may apply to the foreign currency markup fee.
6. Interest on Unpaid Credit Card Balances
- Monthly Interest Rate: 3.50%.
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR): 42% per annum.
- Applicability: Interest is charged when the cardholder does not pay the total outstanding amount by the payment due date.
- Interest Calculation: Interest is calculated on the applicable outstanding balance according to the cardholder agreement.
- New Purchases: The interest-free period may be suspended when the previous statement balance remains unpaid.
- Minimum Amount Due: 5% of the total outstanding balance or ₹200, whichever is higher, subject to the total amount payable.
7. Taxes and Additional Conditions
- GST: Applicable GST, assumed to be 18% for this fictional schedule, will be added wherever applicable.
- All fee amounts listed above are exclusive of applicable taxes.
- Fee waivers are subject to the bank's eligibility rules and transaction classification.
- The bank reserves the right to revise its fee structure with appropriate customer notification and regulatory compliance.
- All charges are governed by the applicable cardholder agreement.
8. Important Policy Disclaimer
This is a fictional product fee schedule created for demonstrating credit-card fee retrieval, policy interpretation, eligibility checking, and financial calculations in a banking knowledge-base project.

The values are illustrative and should not be treated as actual charges applicable to any real bank or credit-card product.
