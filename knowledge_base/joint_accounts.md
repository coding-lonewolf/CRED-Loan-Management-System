# Joint-Account Rules

A joint account has two or more holders, and the mandate defines how transactions, instructions, and access are authorized. The available operating modes may include joint operation or specified survivor/holder arrangements, subject to the institution's terms and applicable law. Adding or removing a holder generally requires identity checks, consent, and approved documentation; one holder should not be assumed to have authority to change the mandate unilaterally. Confirm the actual mandate and product rules before advising on withdrawals, closure, or changes.
