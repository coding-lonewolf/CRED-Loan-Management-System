# Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type

Document Type: Loan Eligibility Policy / Knowledge-Base Article
Market: India
Policy Status: Illustrative reference only
Version: 1.0
Description
Loan eligibility depends on the loan product, applicant profile, repayment capacity, and the lender's current credit policy. For a personal loan, lenders may assess the applicant's age, verified monthly income, employment type and stability, credit history, existing EMIs, debt-to-income obligations, and requested loan amount. Home-loan assessment may additionally consider the property's legal title, approved plans, valuation, applicant's contribution or down payment, and the proposed loan-to-value ratio. For an auto loan, relevant factors may include income, credit profile, vehicle cost and type, down payment, vehicle valuation, and applicable registration or documentation requirements. Education loans may be assessed using the course, institution, admission status, total education cost, future employment prospects, repayment capacity, and a co-applicant or collateral where required by the lender's policy. Business-loan assessment may consider business vintage, ownership, turnover, cash flow, profitability, bank statements, tax returns, financial statements, existing liabilities, loan purpose, and available security. Across products, lenders may verify identity, address, income, employment or business details, and submitted documents. These criteria are illustrative examples for a learning project and are not official Cred or lender policy. Meeting one or more criteria does not guarantee approval, a specific loan amount, or an interest rate. Final eligibility and sanction decisions must be made by an authorized lender using its current product policy, verification results, and applicable laws and regulations.
Eligibility Factors by Loan Type
Loan type	Common assessment factors
Personal loan	Age, verified income, employment stability, credit history, existing EMIs, repayment capacity, requested amount
Home loan	Income, credit history, existing obligations, down payment, property valuation, legal title, approved property documents
Auto loan	Income, credit history, vehicle cost and type, down payment, valuation, repayment capacity
Education loan	Course and institution, admission status, education expenses, co-applicant where required, future repayment capacity, collateral where applicable
Business loan	Business vintage, turnover, cash flow, profitability, financial statements, bank statements, existing liabilities, loan purpose, security


Important Disclaimer
This document contains fictional, illustrative eligibility factors for a banking capstone project. It does not define official Cred, bank, or NBFC eligibility rules. Do not state that an applicant is approved or eligible based only on these general factors. If a product-specific threshold, age range, minimum income, business vintage, or document requirement is not available in an approved current policy, explain that it must be confirmed with the lender.
