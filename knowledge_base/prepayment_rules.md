# Prepayment and Foreclosure Rules

Prepayment means paying part of the outstanding principal before the scheduled due date; foreclosure means settling the remaining loan balance early. Charges and restrictions can depend on the loan type, whether the borrower is an individual or business, the interest-rate type, the agreement date, and applicable regulatory requirements. Before quoting a penalty, verify the signed agreement and current approved policy for that specific product. A support agent must not assume that all loans have a penalty or that all loans are penalty-free.
