# Minimum-Balance Requirements

Minimum-balance rules, where applicable, depend on the account type, location, customer category, and the institution's current terms. Some account types may have no minimum-balance requirement, while others may define an average monthly or quarterly balance and associated charges. Any quoted threshold or fee must be verified against the current official schedule and applicable regulatory directions. If the account type or tariff is unknown, ask a clarifying question or direct the customer to an authorized source instead of guessing.
