# Interest-Rate Slabs

Document Type: Loan Pricing Policy / Knowledge-Base Article
Market: India
Rate Basis: Annual percentage rate (p.a.)
Version: 1.0
Description
Interest rates for loans are determined by the lender's product policy and the applicant's assessed risk profile. Pricing may depend on the loan type, requested amount, repayment tenure, credit history, credit score, income level, employment stability, existing obligations, and the availability and quality of collateral. A rate card should clearly state the applicable annual rate, whether it is fixed or floating, the effective date, eligible customer segment, loan amount and tenure bands, and any conditions or fees that affect the offer. For floating-rate loans, the benchmark, spread, reset frequency, and applicable reset terms should also be disclosed. A rate shown for one product or customer segment must not be assumed to apply to another. This demonstration knowledge base contains no official bank or lender rates and makes no binding rate promise. If a customer requests an exact current rate and an approved, current rate card is not available in the retrieved knowledge base, the system must explain that the rate needs confirmation through an authorized lender source. It must not estimate, fabricate, or present an illustrative rate as an approved offer.
Rate-Slab Reference Fields
Field	Required information
Product	Loan or credit product name
Loan amount band	Minimum and maximum eligible amount
Tenure band	Permitted repayment period
Annual interest rate	Rate expressed as % per annum
Rate type	Fixed or floating
Effective date	Date from which the rate card applies
Eligibility conditions	Credit, income, employment, or collateral requirements
Floating-rate details	Benchmark, spread, and reset frequency, where applicable


Important Disclaimer
No official interest-rate slabs are provided in this document. Exact rates must be obtained from the lender's current, authorized rate card or written offer. Do not infer a customer's approved rate from general eligibility information.
